To “book” a looter, grab a rap sheet and fill it out by following these steps:

---
## 1. State Your Name.
…or at least the name you give to the cops.

---

A **BRIGAND**, a **HERETIC**, a **THIEF**, or a **WITCH**.

## 3. From Which Jurisdiction?
Choose the precinct you’re from:

**DOCKSHORE**, **QUESTERS**, **TEMPLETON**, **SCAFFOLDING**, **MIDDENMIRE**, or **THE HIGH CITY**.

Read more about the precincts on page 10.

---
## 4. Establish a Motive.
Why risk your life and freedom by looting? You can tap into your motive to reroll dice.

Read more about motive & morale on page 13.

---
## 5. Reveal Your Methods.

Methods start at 1 (six-sided) die. Choose one strong method (2 dice) and one weak method (0 dice) from your charge’s list.

---
## 6. Note Your Specialties.
You get bonus dice from your experience in certain situations. Pick two specialties from your charge and two from your precinct.

---
## 7. Behavioral Profile.
You can get bonus dice from your personality quirks. Take one from charge and one from precinct.

---
## 8. Recount Your Exploits.
Exploits are little ways you can break the (game) rules. Start with just the one from your charge.

---
## 9. Collect Your Possessions.
Take all the kit listed with your charge. Choose one keepsake to take from your precinct. You can buy and trade for more from your accomplices.

---
## 10. Name Your Accomplices.
Your accomplices risk life and limb to help you sell off your highly-illegal loot. They can also share juicy rumors that help keep you on track. Record both of the accomplices from your charge.
Find them on pages 25–29.

---
## 11. Face Your Accuser.
Who first ratted you out to the law? Who seeks to thwart you at every turn? Give them a name and a title. You’ll meet them again in Book II: Life of Crime.
